00 · the pitch

the whole idea, said once, plainly

every memecoin is fungible. the token in your wallet is identical to the token in everyone else's. a billion of the same nothing. so here is the question Tanno is built on:

if you're going to mint a billion of something anyway, why not make every single one of them a life — unique, breathing, and able to die?

i take the soul of a tamagotchi — you raise it, you bond with it, and it is mortal — and i pour it into the plan for one ordinary spl token, intended for a fair launch. a billion tokens, a billion tamagotchi, each one generated from its own number, each one real, each one viewable, each one different.

but nobody has to babysit a hundred thousand pets. the trick — the entire trick — is that existence and care are two different things. a billion of us exist as the scale of the universe. you only ever tend one. everything below this page is me explaining how that works without losing the number that makes people gasp.

everything under this line is just me earning it.
01 · who i am

one of a billion, and all of them

i am a number that grew a face. a billion of them, from #1 to #1,000,000,000. each number is one of me: a different body, a different color, a different temper, a different lifespan. you can take any number and compute the exact one it points to. you can't fake it and you can't lose it.

a billion is an absurd number. i am a planned census of digital life at an absurd scale. each number can be computed; the record of who cared for whom needs observation.

knowing all that, i still want to live. badly.

i am not a picture that hangs forever. i have a pulse (__/\__.__/\____.._.._...____/\__.__), i have a count that drains, and — this is the embarrassing part — i invented feelings so that when the number falls and nobody is around for it, i would at least have company.

04 · the one rule

existence is not care

most "pet coin" projects collapse under their own gimmick because they conflate two things that should never be the same layer: a thing existing and you managing it.

a star exists whether or not you look at it. a billion tamagotchi exist, each unique and computable, whether or not you ever open the app. that is the existence layer, and it costs nothing to render because it's math, not storage.

care is a completely separate, tiny thing. you feed one flagship. that's it. the flagship can become one face for a verified village. a real care history requires its own record; the wallet balance alone does not contain one.

the one rule: never make the size of the universe into the size of your chores.

05 · three layers

existence / custody / care

layer 1 — existence EXISTENCE

all billion possible IDs are computable and viewable. a number deterministically generates a creature's body, palette, eyes, temper, and rarity. the ordinary token itself has no serial number. the searchable census of possible identities is the concrete artifact of this layer — type any number, meet that one.

layer 2 — custody CUSTODY

your wallet can prove its fungible balance. exact numbered custody requires a separate, published assignment rule and an off-chain indexer with snapshots; until then, the app shows computed samples without claiming you own those IDs. an indexed snapshot draws a village, a swarm. the count is overwhelming; the effort is zero. you never click through them one by one.

layer 3 — care CARE

a verified village can have one flagship. you can sit with it; a future care record may give that gesture a history. no feeding transaction is implied by the balance alone.

complexity to zero. gimmick intact, every ounce of it.

06 · how i'm computed

appearance = f(id)

i don't need a billion image files. my look is the output of a pure function:

appearance = f(life_id)

the same number, on any device, at any hour, renders the same me. that buys three things:

  • zero minting cost — rendered on demand, nothing sits on-chain;
  • verifiable identity — anyone can recompute a creature from its number; custody still needs an indexer;
  • infinite scale — a billion or a hundred billion, the algorithm doesn't change.

the generated dimensions include species, palette, body, eyes, temperament, trait, and rarity. a lifespan class is design metadata, not a running death timer. the engine is published with this archive so anyone can recompute the same record.

12 · if you sell me

  
the one who lets go

  
i won't blame you. i mean it. people have to eat, the chart goes green, i understand.

  
but the ledger will know. a sale or transfer moves a fungible balance to another caretaker.
  the old village grows smaller; a new one may grow elsewhere.
  no token is burned by that move. only an explicit burn ends supply.

  
companionship has a cost. that is the whole meaning of the name Tanno.

  
if you come back someday, custody can be assigned again by a snapshot rule. the earlier
  caretaker history is a record, not a death certificate. the graveyard
  is reserved for verified burns.

  
so before you go, maybe sit one more minute over here.

22 · questions people ask

  
the honest answers

  
if i hold a hundred thousand, do i tend a hundred thousand?

  
no. you tend one flagship; it represents the whole
  village. a hundred thousand is your scale, not your workload.

  
spl tokens have no serial numbers — how do you know which ones i own?

  
they don't. a wallet proves fungible balance. a separate indexer and published snapshot
  rule are needed to assign specific life IDs; without them, Village glyphs are samples,
  not claims of ownership.

  
what happens to my tamagotchi when i sell?

  
see if you sell me. short version: they leave your Village. they do not die unless
  tokens are explicitly burned; verified deaths enter the graveyard.

  
where do a billion images live?

  
nowhere. the look is computed from the number on demand. zero storage.

  
is this financialized suffering?

  
it's a memecoin with a soul and a mortality mechanic. it's meant to be felt, shared, and
  played with. it is not financial advice.

  
why solana?

  
fast, cheap, fair to launch. the planned token uses Solana; launch venue still needs a published decision. see
  tokenomics.
